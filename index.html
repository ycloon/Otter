Who Are Otters?

Otters are carnivorous mammals in the subfamily Lutrinae. With 13 species across various aquatic environments, they are known for their playful behavior, incredible swimming skills, and thick, waterproof fur coats.

🦦 Social & Playful

Many otters live in family groups called families or rafts. They love sliding down mud banks and juggling rocks!

🌿 Keystone Species

Sea otters play a vital role in marine ecosystems by controlling sea urchin populations, keeping kelp forests healthy.

💤 Hand-Holding Sleepers

Sea otters hold hands while sleeping so they don't drift apart from each other in the ocean currents.
