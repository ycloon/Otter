What is an Otter?

Otters are playful mammals that belong to the Mustelidae family, which also includes weasels, badgers and ferrets.

They are famous for their excellent swimming abilities, intelligence and playful behaviour.

Otters can be found in rivers, lakes, wetlands and even oceans around the world.

Otter Questions

Click a question to reveal the answer.

Do otters really hold hands?

Yes! Sea otters may hold hands while resting in the water to help prevent themselves from drifting apart.

Can otters use tools?

Yes. Sea otters are famous for using rocks to break open hard shells and access their food.

What do otters eat?

Depending on the species, otters may eat fish, crabs, shellfish, frogs and other small aquatic animals.

Are otters good swimmers?

Absolutely! Otters have streamlined bodies and strong tails that make them excellent swimmers.
